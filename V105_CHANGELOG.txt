FEASTERVILLE POS V105 — INVENTORY TRANSFERS — 27 Sep 2026

Base: V104.2 Management Dashboard Top Tab.

Added:
- inventory-transfer.html
- INVENTORY TRANSFERS navigation in the main POS inventory menu.
- Inventory Transfers link in Production Input Administration.
- Transfer workflow: Dispatch -> Pending/In Transit -> Receive/Confirm.
- Cancel Pending Transfer restores stock to the sending store.
- Transfer reference, users, timestamps, notes and status audit trail.
- Manager access across stores; Inventory Controller limited to assigned-store dispatch/receipt controls.
- Production Input transfer only in V105. Sellable-item transfer is intentionally deferred until sellable stock movement tracking is unified.

Stock/accounting design:
- Dispatch creates an internal negative stock movement at source.
- Receive creates an internal positive stock movement at destination.
- Cost basis travels with the stock for valuation.
- Month-End Purchase Cost excludes internal transfers so transfers do not inflate purchases.
- Management Dashboard ignores internal transfers when checking missing supplier purchase costs.
- Transfers do not create sales, operating expenses or revenue.

Database setup:
1. Open Supabase SQL Editor for the Feasterville / Point Of Sale project.
2. Run FEASTERVILLE_POS_V105_INVENTORY_TRANSFERS.sql once.
3. Upload the V105 files to the existing GitHub/site root, preserving CNAME.
4. Sign in and test with a small quantity first.
