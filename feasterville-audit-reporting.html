Current Scope / Limitations

  • Financial year-end is configured as 28 February.
  • Feasterville is currently configured as not VAT registered; VAT201 is therefore not included.
  • Payroll/PAYE/UIF/SDL remains outside Feasterville for now.
  • Opening bank, loans, capital/equity, fixed assets, creditors and debtors remain outside Feasterville and must be loaded later before the Statement of Financial Position and Cash Flow can be final.
  • Stock receipts are retained as an audit schedule and are not automatically posted to the GL yet, avoiding unsupported assumptions about supplier liability/payment classification.
