Management alerts

Estimated result = recorded sales − recorded production-input cost of sales − daily allocated rent and salaries − additional recorded operating expenses. Where input costs or usage are incomplete, the result is provisional and may overstate profit. Rent, wages and inventory purchases recorded as expenses are excluded from the additional deduction to prevent double-counting. Break-even and 10% profit targets use the selected variable-cost assumption as planning targets only; actual profit uses recorded input consumption. Electricity and maintenance recorded during cash-up are included. Other unrecorded expenses are excluded. Missing records are not evidence of zero sales.
