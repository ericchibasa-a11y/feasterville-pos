FEASTERVILLE POS V106.5 — BANKING NUMBER FORMAT
27 Sep 2026

- Banking & Owner Funds Control now formats currency consistently with the Management Dashboard.
- Thousands are separated with spaces and cents use a comma.
- Example: R54 898,60 instead of R54898.60.
- Applied to summary cards, reconciliation table, settlements history and owner drawings history.
- No database or SQL changes required.
