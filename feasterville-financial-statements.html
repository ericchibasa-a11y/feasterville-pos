Draft Accounting Notes

Basis of preparation: Management draft prepared from Feasterville's operational accounting records. The reporting framework remains to be confirmed with the accountant before statutory issue.

Revenue: Revenue is recognised from completed POS sales and is classified between food, ice cream, drinks and other applicable sales categories.

Inventory and Cost of Sales: Inventory purchases are treated as stock movements rather than operating expenses. Cost of Sales is based on production-input recipe consumption using available historical purchase costs, with month-end stock variances incorporated through the Month-End Closure process.

VAT: Feasterville is currently configured as not VAT registered.

Payroll: Payroll, PAYE, UIF and SDL remain outside Feasterville and may be incorporated later.
